  2. hint, the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first (issue 2). The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the date day first. The parser is not involved, so the parser fix does not touch it. Only reports that came in through the form, from tenants on booking-v3. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired; issue 2 is closed as not planned.

That points at time zones or the day boundary, not at date order. The desk already listed them in desk-notes.json. Open: the engineering update on #4471 does not cover them.
